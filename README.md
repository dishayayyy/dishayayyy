CS undergrad specialising in AI — I build things, break them, and then spend an unreasonable amount of time figuring out why.
I want to build things that matter, understand how models actually think, and eventually do research that moves something forward. these aren't separate goals, they're just the same obsession at different zoom levels.

## what's currently happening

- 🔨 **building** — ML projects at the intersection of prediction and interpretability
- 📖 **reading** — papers on AI fairness, bias in models, and why "accurate" doesn't always mean "right"
- 🕳️ **rabbit hole** — how language models actually represent meaning (send help)

## what I think about (a lot)

- fairness in AI isn't a feature — it's a design requirement that keeps getting treated like a feature
